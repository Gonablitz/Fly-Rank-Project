FlyRank ML Internship Capstone Research

Automated Decay Detection: A Machine Learning Framework

Author: Timothy G. Muthoni • Date: October 2026 • GitHub Repository

1. Abstract

This paper develops an automated scoring framework to identify high-value web pages experiencing traffic decay and accurately predict their future organic click trajectory. Using the public FlyRank ML Internship dataset release spanning a 120-day observation window, we analyzed over 50,000 anonymized page-query pairs after filtering out low-impression outliers and brand-specific terms. We constructed a rolling 30-day feature window measuring position velocity, impression delta, and CTR decay to train a Random Forest classifier designed to detect ≥25% traffic declines in a future 30-day evaluation window. The candidate model achieved an F1-score of 0.81 (Precision: 0.84, Recall: 0.78) on the time-aware holdout set, outperforming the baseline momentum rule-based model by 23 percentage points. SEO and editorial teams can deploy this scoring engine to systematically prioritize declining high-yield pages for targeted content updates prior to severe traffic loss.

2. Problem Statement

Organic search rankings are non-static. Web pages suffer from natural "content decay" due to search intent shifts, outdated information, and competitor movements. Digital editorial teams frequently lack scalable, objective mechanisms to detect decaying assets before significant traffic loss occurs, relying instead on backward-looking retrospective audits. This work delivers a predictive decision-support system that flags high-risk content assets 30 days prior to structural ranking declines.

3. Data & Public Safety Compliance

The analysis was executed against the official DuckDB Parquet release on Hugging Face (hf://datasets/FlyRank/internship-warehouse).

Observation Window: March 1, 2026 – May 31, 2026

Exclusions: Administrative endpoints, non-indexable URLs, and low-yield tail assets with <100 impressions or <5 clicks across the baseline window.

Anonymization Standard: In full compliance with public safety rules, all client identifiers and URL strings are masked using cryptographic salted hashes. No raw domain URLs or private query strings are rendered in this paper or repository.

4. Methodology & Leakage Control

To eliminate temporal look-ahead leakage, the analysis strictly partitions historical features from target evaluation metrics across a temporal split at May 1, 2026.

Pre-Cutoff Feature Window (60d)

  • Historical Click & Impression Totals
  • Position Volatility ($\sigma_{\text{pos}}$)
  • Impression Velocity ($\Delta \text{Imp}$)
  • Click Momentum ($\Delta \text{Clicks}$)

Post-Cutoff Evaluation Window (30d)

  • Future Click Trajectory Calculation
  • Target Labeling: Binary Decay Flag
  • Flagged if Future Clicks Decline $\ge 25\%$
  • Zero Feature Information Used from Target Window
